About AI Quest

Making AI make sense

AI Quest is an interactive learning portal that demystifies artificial intelligence for high school students (grades 9–12). It teaches how AI works, how to use AI tools effectively, and how to use them ethically in school.

Our goal and mission

Understand

Replace AI hype and mystery with clear, plain-language foundations and hands-on examples.

Apply

Practice with real AI tool categories and proven techniques, like prompting and fact-checking.

Act with integrity

Build habits for honesty, privacy, credit and fairness so AI supports learning and never replaces it.

Education leaders increasingly treat AI literacy as a core competency instead of an optional extra (World Economic Forum, 2025), and international groups such as UNESCO and the OECD with the European Commission have published frameworks describing what students should know. AI Quest turns those ideas into something students actually want to open: short lessons, games, streaks and badges.

How the site works

  1. Pick up the course. The Course page shows four levels and a final application. Each level has a lesson, a challenge and a knowledge check.
  2. Follow the loop: Learn → Challenge → Quiz → Earn XP. Lessons are interactive. Quizzes give instant feedback, and any question you miss comes back once at the end.
  3. Level up. Every activity earns XP (lesson 50, challenge 75, quiz 100, level bonus 200, daily challenge 25, final application 300). Finishing a level unlocks the next.
  4. Collect badges. There are 10, from AI Explorer to AI Navigator. Some reward skill (a perfect quiz), some reward habits (a three-day streak).
  5. Track your progress. Your Dashboard shows your level, XP bar, streak, badges, weekly XP, daily challenge and high scores.
  6. Practice for fun. The Learn page has four games and practice quizzes for every level.
  7. Earn the certificate. Finish all four levels and the Final Application to become an AI Navigator and print your certificate.

The research behind the curriculum

AI Quest’s curriculum was designed using published AI-literacy frameworks, so it covers what experts say students need. Full citations are on the Resources page.

AI4K12 Big IdeaWhere students meet it
1. PerceptionLevel 1: how computers “sense” the world with cameras and microphones
2. Representation & reasoningLevel 1: how AI organizes information and reasons, like a map app finding a route
3. LearningLevel 1 (machine learning and data) and Level 2 (how data creates bias)
4. Natural interactionLevel 1 (chatbots and language) and Level 3 (talking to AI with prompts)
5. Societal impactLevel 2 (bias, misinformation) and Level 4 (integrity, privacy, fairness)
UNESCO competency ideaHow AI Quest uses it
Four dimensions: human-centred mindset, ethics of AI, AI techniques and applications, AI system designLevel 2 (human judgment stays in charge) and Level 4 (ethics) cover the first two. Levels 1 and 3 cover techniques and applications. AI system design appears only lightly (for example, how data shapes a model), because building AI systems is beyond this course.
Three progression levels: Understand, Apply, CreateLevels 1–2 focus on Understand. Levels 3–4 focus on Apply. The Final Application asks students to Create a prompt and write their own AI Use Statement.

The OECD and European Commission AI literacy framework describes AI literacy as the knowledge, skills and attitudes to understand how AI works, evaluate its outputs and use it ethically and creatively. That goal shaped the balance of Levels 1–4. Common Sense Education’s Digital Literacy & Well-Being curriculum for grades 9–12 informed our everyday scenarios about privacy, misinformation and online behavior.

Design choices

Inspiration

We were inspired by the way Duolingo builds daily habits (XP, streaks, a friendly mascot and bite-size lessons that give instant feedback) and by Khan Academy’s focus on mastery and clear progress tracking. All of AI Quest’s art, code and content are our own.

Green and red appear only for correct and incorrect feedback, always alongside words and icons so color is never the only signal.

Typography and icons

Headings use Lilita One, a rounded, friendly display font. Body text uses Nunito for easy reading on screens. Lessons follow a consistent hierarchy: big question or title, short explanation, then interaction. Icons are simple, consistent line icons, and Byte the robot appears throughout to guide students, just as a mascot guides learners in other popular apps.

Accessibility and compatibility

  • High-contrast text, visible keyboard focus, a skip link, and number-key shortcuts for answer choices.
  • Feedback is announced to screen readers, and animations stop for visitors who prefer reduced motion.
  • The layout adapts from phones to desktops, with a bottom tab bar on small screens.
  • Built with standard HTML, CSS and JavaScript that work in the latest versions of Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari.

Privacy and safety

AI Quest has no accounts, no logins, no ads and no tracking. Your name (optional), XP, badges and notes are saved only in your own browser on your own device. Nothing is sent to a server. You can erase everything any time with “Reset my progress” on the Dashboard. Because progress is saved per browser, using a different device or browser starts you fresh.

How it was built

AI Quest is hand-coded with HTML5, CSS3 and plain JavaScript. We did not use a website builder or a content management system. We also did not use a JavaScript framework. All lessons are stored as structured data and turned into interactive screens by a small activity player we wrote. The site is hosted as static files, so it is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. A full list of sources, tools and AI disclosures is on the Resources page.
